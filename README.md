# Goutly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 September 2026_

Goutly ("the app") is a gout and purine tracker. It is designed to be private by
default: your health logs stay on your device. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your data.

## Data we collect

**None that identifies you.** The developer does not collect your personal
health records. Specifically:

- **No accounts and no sign-in.**
- **No analytics or tracking** of any kind.
- **No advertising** and no ad networks.
- **No third-party SDKs** that collect data.
- Your uric-acid readings, food log, and flare diary are stored on your
  device (via Apple's on-device storage) and are never uploaded to us. You may
  choose to export and share copies using Reports, as described below.

## Food barcode scanning

When you use the optional barcode **food scanner**, the barcode you scan is sent to
**Open Food Facts** (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food-product database, solely
to look up that product's name so Goutly can estimate its purine content. Only the
barcode is sent — no personal information, and no data about you. Reports purchase and restore requests also communicate with Apple. See Open Food Facts' own terms and
privacy information at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/ .

## Reports and sharing

In versions that include Reports, previews and PDF/CSV files are generated on your
device from the date range you select. Temporary export files are removed after
the sharing flow releases them. If you choose a sharing destination, the file is
provided to that app or service and may leave your device. Copies you save or send
are controlled by you and the recipient or service; Goutly cannot delete those
copies. Review the report and destination before sharing health information.

## Purchases

The optional one-time Reports unlock uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit for
product pricing, purchase verification and restoring access. Apple processes the
payment; the developer does not receive your payment-card details. Goutly does not
send the contents of your health logs to Apple as part of a purchase or restore
request. App Store transactions are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## Health information & disclaimer

Goutly is an **informational tool, not a medical device**, and not a substitute for
professional medical advice. Purine values are approximate and for awareness only.
Goutly does not use Apple HealthKit and does not read or write your Health data.

## Children's privacy

Goutly is intended for adults managing their own health and is not directed at
children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
