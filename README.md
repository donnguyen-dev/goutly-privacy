# Goutly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 July 2026_

Goutly ("the app") is a gout and purine tracker. It is designed to be private by
default: your health logs stay on your device. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your data.

## Data we collect

**None that identifies you.** Goutly does not collect, store, or share any personal
information. Specifically:

- **No accounts and no sign-in.**
- **No analytics or tracking** of any kind.
- **No advertising** and no ad networks.
- **No third-party SDKs** that collect data.
- Your uric-acid readings, food log, and flare diary are stored **only on your
  device** (via Apple's on-device storage) and are never uploaded to us.

## Food barcode scanning

When you use the optional barcode **food scanner**, the barcode you scan is sent to
**Open Food Facts** (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food-product database, solely
to look up that product's name so Goutly can estimate its purine content. Only the
barcode is sent — no personal information, and no data about you. If you never use
the scanner, the app makes no network requests. See Open Food Facts' own terms and
privacy information at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/ .

## Health information & disclaimer

Goutly is an **informational tool, not a medical device**, and not a substitute for
professional medical advice. Purine values are approximate and for awareness only.
Goutly does not use Apple HealthKit and does not read or write your Health data.

## Children's privacy

Goutly is intended for adults managing their own health and is not directed at
children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
